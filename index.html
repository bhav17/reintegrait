Why it matters

A second chance starts in the first week

Coming home from prison is a fresh start. Whether it holds often depends on the first few days: a bed, an ID, a refill, a first shift at work. When the referral lands, a returning individual walks in, is accepted and starts building again.

ReintegrAIt helps case workers make that first match the right one.

About the company

Referrals fail on rules nobody writes down

When someone comes home from prison, the first weeks decide a lot: a bed, an ID, medication, a job. Case managers make those referrals by phone and memory. A program can be full this week, close intake on Fridays, or quietly not take a certain offense. None of that is in a directory, so returning individuals make wasted trips and case managers spend hours calling.

ReintegrAIt learns which services actually accept which returning individuals, from the outcomes case managers already know, and keeps that knowledge current. It also gives back the time spent typing notes after every visit.

We are a student venture from the Building an AI Company course (43120), building and testing the product with Chicago case managers, provider intake staff and program directors.

Product overview

One loop, from visit to outcome

The AI does the language and the ranking. The case manager approves, picks and records. Every outcome makes the next ranking better.

Step 1

Record a voice memo

After the visit, the case manager dictates for a minute or two. Speech-to-text keeps a timestamp on every line.

Case manager speaks
Step 2

Approve the case note

The AI drafts a note where every fact cites the recording. Unsure facts are flagged. Nothing saves until it is approved.

AI drafts · you approve
Step 3

See ranked matches

For each need, the top services ranked by chance of acceptance, with reasons, distance and how fresh the data is.

AI ranks · you pick
Step 4

Refer and close the loop

Send with the returning individual’s consent. When the appointment passes, one tap records whether they showed up and were accepted.

You send · both learn

When the AI is unsure

Built to say “check this”

Speech recognition mishears, and listings go stale. The product shows its uncertainty instead of hiding it, and it keeps a few lines it never crosses.

Check flags

A date said with “I think”, or a neighborhood that isn’t one of the 77 community areas, is flagged.

Confirm key facts

Name, release date and top need are ticked before a note can be approved.

Call first

Old or thin data means low confidence. Send stays off until the case manager calls or gives a reason.

No risk scores

It ranks services, never people. No parole contact, no sharing without their consent.
